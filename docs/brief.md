# Shared Brief for Issues 1–4

This file is the single briefing document for all four sequential issues. Read it
instead of being re-briefed.

## Durable context

**Site:** https://tezsayt.dev
**Business:** A two-person web agency in Baku, Azerbaijan. We build a real, live
homepage for an Azerbaijani SMB *before* they pay anything. If they like it, we build
the full site. Accounts are created in the client's name; they can reset the passwords
and remove us at any moment.

**Audience:** Non-technical Baku business owners who get customers via word of mouth
and Instagram, not Google. Many have been burned — agencies that were slow and
expensive, freelancers who took a deposit and vanished. They want one person handling
everything and a price they can see before they call.

**Verticals:** dental clinics, tutoring schools, law firms, cafes, repair shops, small
importers.

**Languages:** Azerbaijani primary, Russian a genuine second, English for credibility.
Baku-focused.

**Differentiators in fixed order:**
1. Free live homepage before payment
2. Full ownership with a concrete exit mechanic
3. Speed as per-tier ranges

Price never leads.

## Competitor landscape

- **Instagram-only** — primary competitor. Profile + WhatsApp link. Beat by showing
  what Instagram can't do: menu with prices, booking, Google Maps, search visibility.
- **"The nephew"** — free/near-free family labor. Sets the price floor.
- **DIY builders** (Wix, Tilda, startme.ws) — English dashboards, no local support.
- **Local agencies/freelancers** — shaftali.az, ibrahimov.az, alovdigital.com,
  masterstudio.az, lalafo.az, xidmetler.az, tezbazar.az.

## Decided §12 items

1. Clock start: deposit received
2. "gün" = calendar days (təqvim günü)
3. Support structure: à la carte (not bundled tiers)
4. Payment: free draft → 50% on approval → 50% at launch
5. Revisions on full build: 3 rounds max (don't count toward timeline)
6. Domain renewal: agency responsibility if client has a care plan
7. Domain registrar account: client's name per §3
8. Care plan cancellation: proactively remove credentials
9. Biznes saytı: AZ+RU standard; EN is a paid add-on
10. "Ən populyar" dropped. Loading claim: "Sürətli yüklənmə" (no sub-second number)
