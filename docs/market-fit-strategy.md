# market-fit-strategy.md
Revised strategy after market-fit review and stress test, reconciled against the live
site as of 2026-09-19. Where this document and the consultant reports disagree, this
document wins. Where this document and the live site disagree, this document is the
instruction to change the site.

## 1. Who we are actually competing against

Not agencies. The live About-page comparison table fights "ənənəvi agentlik" — the
wrong opponent. The real decision in the owner's head is one of:

- **Instagram-only** — they already have a "website": a profile with a WhatsApp link.
  This is the primary competitor. Beating it means showing what Instagram cannot do:
  a menu with prices, booking, Google Maps presence, appearing in search.
- **"The nephew"** — free or near-free family labor. This sets the price floor, not the
  agencies. A site that looks cheap loses to free every time.
- **DIY builders** (Wix, Tilda, startme.ws) — fail locally on English dashboards, no
  local support, and the owner still writing their own bilingual content. Our edge is
  that we remove the work, not that the tool is better.
- **Other local agencies and freelancers** — shaftali.az, ibrahimov.az,
  alovdigital.com, masterstudio.az, plus the classified marketplaces (lalafo.az,
  xidmetler.az, tezbazar.az). Present, but not the first thought.

REQUIRED CHANGE: at least one prominent comparison on the site must be against
Instagram-only. The agency comparison may stay but must not be the only one, and must
not anchor 2000+ AZN as "the bad price" while we sell a 2000 AZN tier (see §6, C4).

## 2. Differentiators, in fixed priority order

1. **Free live homepage before payment.** Nobody else in this segment does this at
   scale. Scope is already defined on the site: ana səhifə plus one revision round.
2. **Full ownership from day one**, with a concrete exit mechanic (§3).
3. **Speed, stated per tier as a range.** Genuine, but third — shaftali.az claims a
   week too, and ibrahimov.az matches our entry price.

Price is NOT a differentiator and must never lead.

## 3. Ownership — settled, do not reopen

REJECTED: the stress test's proposal that we remain registered technical administrator
by default. That would falsify the live claim and trade our strongest differentiator
for revenue we don't yet have.

**Settled model.** Every account — hosting, domain, analytics, anything else — is
created **in the client's name**. We hold working credentials at their discretion, for
as long as they want us on the site. To remove us, they reset the passwords. That is
the entire exit process: no request, no handover, no leverage, no waiting on us.

Why this works: ownership stays absolute, so the trust claim survives intact, and
recurring revenue rests on convenience rather than control. Nothing is locked; they
simply have no reason to bother unless they want to leave.

Copy rules:
- Never describe our access as administration, management or control. We hold working
  credentials at the client's discretion.
- State the exit mechanic concretely, at least once: "Hesablar sizin adınızadır.
  İstədiyiniz an şifrələri sıfırlayıb bizi çıxara bilərsiniz." The specific mechanic is
  what makes it believable to someone who has been burned — a vague "you can leave
  anytime" is weaker.
- The care plan is sold on response speed and on not having to touch anything. Never
  on holding keys.
- This gets its own section on the site, not just an FAQ line. No competitor in the
  landscape offers it, and masterstudio.az hosting on their own servers is precisely
  what it answers.

## 4. Speed claims — one rule

Turnaround is advertised as the **range for the specific tier**, never as a single
site-wide number. Every single-number claim currently live is to be removed or replaced
(§6).

Every range must be accompanied, at least once per page where it appears, by the
clock-start condition. PENDING (§12): what starts the clock, and whether "gün" means
iş günü or təqvim günü. Until decided these are placeholders, not guesses.

No guarantee language ("zəmanət", "гарантия", "guaranteed"), no penalty or refund
clause, no countdown or urgency framing. These are estimates and must read as such.

## 5. Tier routing by vertical

The entry tier (Vizitkart, 500 AZN) must not anchor the verticals that need more.
- Dental clinics, law firms, tutoring schools, consultancies → **Biznes saytı**. Their
  vertical pages quote that tier's price and range, not the entry tier's.
- Cafés, repair shops, single-service businesses → Vizitkart where appropriate.
- Product sellers, importers → Onlayn mağaza.

Underpricing hurts in clinics and law firms specifically; visible cheapness reads as
low quality in Baku's professional services.

## 7. Portfolio and credibility

The portfolio is what two founders have: Saleh Tech School, Xsolla Game Recap,
Kraamzorg, MindMorph, Zelix Topup, Infinity Investment, Meridiana. **Keep it.** Almost
nobody clicks through all seven, and the relevant ones are surfaced on the main page.

Two requirements, not a rebuild:
- **Ordering.** Whatever most resembles a Baku SMB leads on the main page.
- **Live-status check.** "Real bizneslər, real saytlar, hazırda yayında" must be true
  of the set. Any project not currently live comes off, or the line changes.

Homepage logo wall (metro, PASHA Hackathon, GDG, IDDA, Holberton, Xsolla): these are
hackathon and community affiliations, and a logo strip reads as a client roster by
convention. Label it once, quietly and accurately — e.g. "Hackathon və icma
tədbirlərindən". Not a disclaimer, not apologetic, just not passing as clients. No
agent may leave it unlabeled.

Until a Baku SMB project exists in the portfolio, credibility is carried by the free
draft itself, the blog (issue 3), and naming the founders openly — which the About page
already does well.

## 8. The AI position

Keep the honesty; change the placement. "We use AI" as an About-page pillar arms the
buyer's strongest objection: if AI builds it, why does it cost 800 manat?

Settled framing: AI removes repetitive build work, which is why the timeline and price
are what they are. Design judgment, structure, local understanding and accountability
are ours. What the client pays for is the finished thing and someone answerable for it.

Placement: the answer to "Bu qədər sürətli necə ola bilərsiniz?" — already a live FAQ
question — not a headline argument for choosing us.

## 9. Language policy

Azerbaijani primary, always. Russian second, not English. For clinics, law firms and
the older, wealthier Baku clientele, a Russian version materially changes perceived
professionalism.

PENDING (§12): the calculator defaults to 1 language and prices translation as a
one-off add-on. For Biznes saytı specifically, consider AZ+RU as standard. Until
decided, do not change the pricing model — flag it.

## 10. Free draft as leakage-prone inventory

Some prospects will take the free homepage to a nephew. Controls to state on the site:
- the draft is delivered as a live link on our infrastructure
- it covers the homepage and one revision round (already stated — keep)
- source transfers at payment; account ownership per §3 at launch

Accept a share of leakage as acquisition cost. Do not add friction that damages the
zero-risk promise.

## 11. Capacity and scarcity

Two founders with classes deliver roughly 3 sites/month in practice. If demand
materializes, slots — not speed — are the constraint.

Scarcity may be published ONLY if true and enforced ("ayda 3 layihə qəbul edirik"),
never as an urgency device. The live "bir neçə saat ərzində cavab veririk" will break
during exam weeks — either narrow it to published hours or remove it.

## 12. Open decisions — not to be guessed by any agent

1. Clock start: contract, deposit, or client content received?
2. "gün" — iş günü or təqvim günü?
3. Support plan structure (C5): which one is real?
4. Payment structure: pay-on-completion, or free draft → 50% on approval → 50% at
   launch? The latter doesn't break zero-risk (risk ends at draft approval) but changes
   the "Ödəniş necə işləyir?" answer. Decide, don't drift.
5. Revision count on the full build (only the free draft's single round is stated).
6. Domain and hosting renewal: who pays, from year two?
7. Does the domain registrar account follow the §3 pattern too?
8. When a client cancels a care plan, do we proactively remove our credentials, or wait
   for them to reset? Stating this on the site, in their favor, is a strong trust
   signal.
9. AZ+RU standard on Biznes saytı, or keep translation as a paid add-on?
10. Is "Ən populyar" provable? Is "1 saniyədən az" measured?

- Treating "7 days" as the brand centerpiece — obsolete; tiering already happened.
- Auditing every portfolio project's client relationship — dropped (§7); only
